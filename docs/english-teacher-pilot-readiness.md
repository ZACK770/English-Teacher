## Learning continuity

- Telephone lesson selection keeps the configured total vocabulary load. When the eligible topic/material has evidence of past practice, reserve up to two slots for the oldest previously scored or exposed targets; always keep at least one focus slot if available. Merely assigning an unheard target does not qualify. Review is not a mastery claim. The original free/paid and material eligibility restrictions are preserved; grammar-only lessons and web selection remain unchanged.
- In the phone prompt, review both previous words in a short authentic exchange, preferably a sentence independently formed by the learner. Do not supply the English answer first. If a learner struggles, scaffold once then proceed. New material remains part of the plan. The existing score history and two-distinct-call mastery requirement are unchanged.
- Independent `save_learner_name` tool for actual telephone learners. The name is verified against the latest learner transcript, either a direct self-identification or response to a name question; it cannot be invented from another caller's roleplay/example. Save it independently from completing intake; never overwrite on unverified model guess. First phone intake is limited to missing name/level, with goal if volunteered, and moves to actual practice promptly.
- In active deterministic coach mode, supplied repetition alone is insufficient for an independent 100. The guard omits such grades rather than marking a zero, saves other valid scores, indicates omitted target IDs to the teacher, and abstains if another potentially independent contextual occurrence exists. It does not judge arbitrary semantic answers or pronunciation. No change to prior saved grades.

Earlier pilot added fixed apology playback on failed cascade and safe provider closure diagnostics. No new test call has happened since that deployment; root provider remains unverified.
